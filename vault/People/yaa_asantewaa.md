Yaa Asantewaa was a queen mother (ohemaa) of the Ashanti people and a principal leader of the War of the Golden Stool in 1900, the final military resistance of the Ashanti Empire against British colonial occupation. A member of the Ashanti royal family, she took command at the town of Nsuta in 1900 when the British exiled the Ashanti king Prempeh I, and led a guerrilla campaign that forced the British to negotiate. She was captured at the end of the war, exiled to the Seychelles until 1901, and thereafter continued to oppose British rule, eventually becoming a leading voice in Ashanti political affairs in the 20th century.
