Wenamuke, known in English-language sources as Wenamuka (also Wenamuke or Wenamuk), was a king (hosi) of the Ndebele people in the mid-19th century. He is the most widely cited source for the tradition that Mzilikazi was born a slave who rose through the ranks of the Mthethwa of Zulu king Shaka, and that he led a large fraction of his followers westward in the 1830s to found the Ndebele kingdom of Matabeleland in what is now Zimbabwe.
